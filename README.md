# AtliQ Hardwares — Sales & Finance Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **sales performance and financial performance of AtliQ Hardwares** using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis focuses on understanding revenue growth, customer performance, country/market contribution, cost of goods sold (COGS), gross margin, and gross margin percentage across fiscal years.

The dashboard/report provides a consolidated view of business performance from **FY2019 to FY2021**, helping identify growth patterns, high-performing markets and customers, and profitability trends.

---

## 📊 Key Analysis

### 1. Customer Sales Performance

The customer-level analysis compares net sales across FY2019, FY2020 and FY2021 and calculates the change between FY2020 and FY2021.

The report includes customers such as Amazon, AtliQ e Store, AtliQ Exclusive, Flipkart, Reliance Digital, Walmart and others.

### 2. Country Performance & Target Analysis

Sales performance is analyzed across multiple countries and compared against FY2021 targets.

For example, India generated **$161.3M** in FY2021 against a **$171M target**, while the overall business generated **$598.9M** against a **$654M target**.

### 3. P&L Analysis

The financial analysis tracks:

* Net Sales
* COGS
* Gross Margin
* Gross Margin %

FY2021 recorded:

* **Net Sales:** $598.9M
* **COGS:** $380.7M
* **Gross Margin:** $218.2M
* **Gross Margin %:** 36.4%

### 4. Monthly & Quarterly Performance

Monthly financial performance is analyzed using fiscal months and quarters, allowing comparison of Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin and GM %.

### 5. Market Profitability

FY2021 market-level analysis compares Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin and GM % across countries.

For example, India generated **$161.3M in sales and $51.6M gross margin**, while the USA generated **$87.8M in sales and $32.5M gross margin**.

## 📈 Key Business Metrics

| Metric       | FY2019 |  FY2020 |  FY2021 |
| ------------ | -----: | ------: | ------: |
| Net Sales    | $87.5M | $196.7M | $598.9M |
| COGS         | $51.2M | $123.4M | $380.7M |
| Gross Margin | $36.2M |  $73.3M | $218.2M |
| GM %         |  41.4% |   37.3% |   36.4% |

The report shows substantial growth in absolute sales and gross margin between FY2019 and FY2021, alongside a decline in GM % from **41.4% to 36.4%**.
